Cheque Printer

Enter the date, payee and amount. The amount is converted to words automatically.

Cheque details

Amount in words-
This bank uses the layout from your Excel template. Date, amount and words are printed in the same format as Excel, so the format options below do not apply.
  1. In the print dialog choose Actual size / 100% and turn off "Fit to page".
  2. For a new cheque book, print a test page on plain paper and hold it behind a cheque against the light.
  3. If anything is off, adjust X/Y in the layout settings or drag the field in the preview.

Preview

Click a field in the preview to edit it

Only the blue text is printed. Click a field to select it. Clicking a date box selects only that box; use "Select all 8 date boxes" to move them together. Drag the field to move it, or drag the orange handles on its edges to make it narrower, wider, shorter or taller. You can also use the arrow buttons (or the keyboard arrow keys, hold Shift for bigger steps) and change the font size. Each arrow moves 0.5 mm. Click Save layout to keep the setting for this bank.

Layout & printer settings:

Use a flat, straight scan or photo cropped exactly to the cheque edges. The photo only helps you line up fields; it is never printed.

X is measured from the left edge and Y from the top edge, in mm, to the text baseline. For the date, X is the centre of the first box and "Box gap" is the distance between boxes.
